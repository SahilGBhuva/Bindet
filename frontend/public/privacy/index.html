Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

bindet is a study app that turns your class notes into flashcards, quizzes and a tutor you can ask questions. This policy explains, in plain language, what information bindet handles, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

bindet is a free student project created for the Congressional App Challenge.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account email, your profile, your notes and the study activity you create.
  • Notes, photos of notes, quiz answers and tutor messages are sent to AI providers to make your study materials. Please don't upload sensitive personal information.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • bindet is meant for students 13 and older. Younger students need a parent, guardian or school to agree first.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time in Settings → Delete account. You can also ask us to see, fix, export or delete your data by emailing officialbindet@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

bindet is built and run by the bindet team, a small group of students ("we", "us"). It is available at bindet-learn.vercel.app. bindet is a free student project created for the Congressional App Challenge, and is not run by a school or company.

If you have a question about this policy or your data, email us at officialbindet@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password. When you sign up with email, your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase Auth, which stores it in hashed form. We never see your password.
  • Google sign-in. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with our sign-in provider. bindet uses your email to identify your account and may use your name as your starting display name. We don't get access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Profile. Your username, display name, friend code (generated by bindet), daily goal, and your privacy settings ("Appear in search" and "Friend requests").

Your study material

  • Notes. When you upload a note (a PDF, Word document, text file or photo), we read the text out of it and store that text, along with the course and unit you filed it under, the file name, the file type and its size. We don't keep the original file or photo; only the extracted text is saved.
  • Flashcards and quiz questions made from your notes, and any study instructions you give the AI (for example "focus on dates").
  • Quiz answers. Your answers are checked, and in some cases sent to an AI to be graded. We save whether you were right and your progress, not the text of your answers. A short-lived cache of grading results is described in section 8.
  • Tutor conversations. Your messages to the tutor and its replies are saved so you can come back to them. If you attach a photo, it is sent to the AI for that reply and is not stored; we only save its file name.

Progress

XP, streaks, accuracy by topic, daily goals and similar statistics that the app calculates as you study.

Friends, groups and messages

  • Friends: friend requests, friendships, friend quests, high-fives and other reactions, blocks you create, and reports you file about other users (including any details you write).
  • Study groups: group names, descriptions, invite codes, membership and roles, group goals and milestones.
  • Group chat: messages and images you send in a study group, plus read receipts and "is typing" status. Chat images are stored in private storage that only members of that group can open.
  • Notifications about friend and group activity.

Tasks

Assignments and tasks you create, including their titles, descriptions, due dates, status, checklists, comments, links you attach, assignees and an activity history for shared group tasks.

Technical information

  • IP address. To stop abuse, the server counts requests per network address for a few minutes at a time. These counts are kept in the server's memory only and are not written to our database.
  • Usage-limit records. To enforce daily limits on uploads, AI requests, friend requests and similar actions, we record which account did which kind of action and when (not what it contained). These records are deleted after about 7 days.
  • Server logs. Our AI features write short operational log lines (such as "flashcards created, 1.2 seconds"). They use a scrambled (hashed) form of your account ID, and never contain your notes, messages, questions or answers. Our hosting provider also keeps standard request logs, which can include IP addresses and browser details.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't collect your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

3. How AI is used

bindet's study features are powered by large language models. To use them, we send the relevant content to an AI provider:

  • the text of your notes, to make flashcards and quiz questions;
  • photos of notes and scanned PDFs, to read the text in them;
  • quiz questions, the correct answer and your answer, to grade an answer;
  • your tutor messages, attached photos, earlier messages in the conversation and the relevant notes, to write a tutor reply;
  • any study instructions you give.

bindet uses Google Gemini models, accessed through OpenRouter, which forwards each request to the company that runs the model. Scanned PDFs may be read with Mistral's OCR service through OpenRouter. OpenRouter may route a request to another AI provider if one is unavailable. We don't send your email address or name with these requests.

Please don't upload sensitive personal information (for example health, financial or government ID details, or other people's private information) in notes, photos or tutor messages. Anything you upload may be processed by AI providers as described here.

AI output can be wrong. Always check important facts against your course materials.

4. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • create your account, sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • store your notes and turn them into flashcards, quizzes and tutor answers;
  • track your progress, streaks and goals;
  • run the social features you choose to use: friends, study groups, group chat and shared tasks;
  • send account emails, such as sign-up confirmation codes and password reset links;
  • prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and keep the service running and fix problems;
  • respond to your requests and reports.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, do not share it for advertising, and do not use it to build advertising profiles. We don't use your notes to train AI models.

5. Who can see your information

  • Only you: your notes, flashcards, quizzes, tutor conversations, personal tasks and your email address.
  • Other bindet users: if "Appear in search" is on, signed-in people can find you by your username or display name and see your username, display name and friend code. Your friends can see your username, display name, friend code and study stats such as XP and streaks. Members of your study groups can see your name, your group activity, the messages and images you send in that group's chat and the group's shared tasks.
  • Service providers that run parts of bindet for us, listed in the next section.
  • When the law requires it. We may share information if we must do so by law, or to protect the safety of a person or the security of the service.

If bindet is ever transferred to someone else, we will tell you before your information is handled under a different privacy policy.

7. Cookies and browser storage

bindet does not set cookies for advertising or tracking. To keep you signed in and remember your preferences, it uses your browser's own storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) on your device. This includes:

  • your sign-in session;
  • recent copies of your data, so pages load quickly;
  • things that only live on your device: a profile picture or course cover images you choose, recent quiz attempts, and study instructions;
  • preferences such as theme, sidebar layout and text size.

When you sign out, bindet removes your session and account data from that browser. Display preferences such as theme stay. You can also clear all of it at any time in your browser settings. The sign-in providers (Supabase and, if you use it, Google) may set their own cookies during sign-in.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and study data stays until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Notes and tutor conversations can be deleted by you at any time in the app. Deleting a note also deletes the flashcards made from it.
  • AI results cache. To avoid paying for the same AI request twice, we keep some AI results for a short time: flashcards, text read from photos or scans, and grading results for up to 30 days, and tutor replies for up to 7 days. Results are looked up by a scrambled fingerprint (a hash) of the input, but a stored result can contain your content, such as the text read from your photo. When you delete a note, the cached flashcards and text made from it are deleted too, unless another note still uses the same entry (yours, or another user's upload of identical content). Deleting a tutor conversation deletes its cached reply. Deleting your account deletes every cached result tied to your account, except shared entries that another user's identical upload still uses. A few results cached before we began linking them to accounts aren't linked to anyone and simply expire on the schedule above.
  • Quiz questions. Recently served quiz questions are kept for 7 days and reusable quiz questions for up to 30 days. Questions made from your own notes or instructions are deleted with your account.
  • Usage-limit records are deleted after about 7 days. In-memory request counts disappear within minutes.
  • Server and hosting logs are kept for the limited period set by our hosting provider.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all your data at any time in Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also email officialbindet@gmail.com and we'll do it within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, profile, notes, flashcards, quizzes, tutor conversations, progress, personal tasks, friends, notifications, and the messages and images you sent in group chats, along with your comments, attachments and assignments on other people's tasks. Study groups you own pass to the member who has been in the group the longest, together with the group tasks you created, and that member is told; a group where you're the only member is deleted. Fragments may remain for a limited period in our hosting providers' logs and backups until those are replaced.

Individual group chat messages can't yet be deleted from inside the app. They're deleted with your account, or you can ask us by email.

9. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal information we have about you;
  • correct information that's wrong (you can also edit your profile in Settings);
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • export a copy of your data in a common format;
  • object to or limit how we use it, or withdraw your consent.

You can delete your account yourself at any time in Settings → Delete account (see section 8). For anything else, email officialbindet@gmail.com from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it's you. We'll complete account deletion requests sent by email within 30 days, and answer other requests within 30 days as well. Copies of deleted data may stay in our providers' logs and backups for a limited time until they are replaced.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we handle your information to provide the service you asked for, for our legitimate interest in keeping bindet safe and working, and with your consent where needed. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have the rights listed above, and we will not treat you differently for using them. We do not sell or "share" personal information for targeted advertising.

You can also control what others see: turn off "Appear in search" or "Friend requests" in Settings, remove friends, block or report people, and leave study groups.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your browser, our servers and our providers;
  • database access rules (row-level security) so signed-in users can only reach their own data and the groups they belong to;
  • private file storage for chat images, opened through short-lived links;
  • keeping secret keys on the server, never in the website's code;
  • rate limits and checks on uploads to prevent abuse.

No online service can be perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee that information will never be accessed without permission. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we'll let you know as the law requires.

11. Children and schools

bindet is made for students and is intended for people 13 and older. Children under 13 may use bindet only with the consent of a parent or guardian, or of their school where the school has authorized it. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information without that consent, we will delete it.

Parents and guardians can contact us at officialbindet@gmail.com to review or delete their child's information, or to stop further collection.

If a school asks students to use bindet, the school is responsible for its students' education records, and we'll work with the school on requests about those records, including under the US Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).

12. Where data is processed

bindet is run from the United States. Our hosting provider runs the app's server in the United States, and our other providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using bindet, your information may be processed outside the country where you live, where privacy laws may differ.
