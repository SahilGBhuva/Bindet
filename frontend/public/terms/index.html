1. Who can use bindet

bindet is for students. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account, unless a parent, guardian or your school has agreed to your use of bindit. If you're under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian, and they should read these terms with you.

If you use bindet for a school or class, your school's rules also apply.

2. Your account

  • Give a real email address you can access, so you can confirm your account and reset your password.
  • Keep your password private and don't share your account. You're responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Pick a username and display name that don't impersonate anyone or offend others.
  • If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and email us at officialbindet@gmail.com.

3. Your content

"Your content" means what you put into bindit: notes, photos, study instructions, quiz answers, tutor messages, tasks, comments, and group chat messages and images.

You own your content. To run bindet, you give us a limited, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to store, copy, process and display your content only as needed to provide the service to you and to the people you choose to share it with. This includes sending it to our AI providers to create flashcards, quizzes, grades and tutor replies, as described in the Privacy Policy. This permission ends when you delete the content or your account, except for copies that take a short time to expire from caches and backups.

Only upload content you have the right to use. Your own class notes are fine. Don't upload material that you are not allowed to share (for example, copies of paid textbooks or tests you were told not to copy) and don't post other people's private information.

Please don't upload sensitive personal information, such as health, financial or government ID details.

4. AI-generated content

Flashcards, quiz questions, grades, text read from photos and tutor replies are made by AI. They are study aids, and they can be incomplete or wrong.

  • Check important information against your teacher, textbook or course materials.
  • AI output isn't professional advice (for example medical, legal or financial advice).
  • Academic integrity is your responsibility. Follow your school's rules about using AI tools. Don't use bindet to cheat on tests or assignments or to turn in work that isn't yours.

You may use the study materials bindet makes for you for your own learning.

You can remove friends, block people, report them, and leave groups at any time. Group owners manage their groups. We aren't able to monitor every message, and we aren't responsible for what other users post, but please tell us about anything harmful at officialbindet@gmail.com.
